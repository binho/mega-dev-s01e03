How each report was placed

The tenant decides the cause, not the intake tag and not the reporter’s browser (src/lib/kb/causes.ts). Every tenant on file writes the day first.

  1. shift: the body says the date moved by one day, or the same meeting landed on different days for people in different places. This is a time-zone problem.
  2. hint: the report came in through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type what it says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. The parser fix does not touch this.
  3. parser: every report not placed under hint, the one-day moves included. The shared parser reads the tenant’s day-first date month first, so day and month flip.

Duplicate rows from overlapping export pages are counted once, by report id. Counts follow the desk’s own view: a report is counted where it is filed, so the shift reports also count under parser, where their tenant files them. The rows add up to more than the ticket for that reason.
